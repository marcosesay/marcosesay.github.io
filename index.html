About me

I'm a Data & AI Solutions Engineer at IBM, where I lead technical sales cycles for enterprise clients across financial services, healthcare, supply chain and technology. I scope the data platform, build the proof of concept, and structure embedded solution agreements that put IBM technology inside clients' own products.

I also build the tools that make that work faster and more reliable: a briefing generator IBM sellers use before client calls, and evaluation suites that show what separates a convincing demo from a production-ready agent.

Before IBM, I worked in data risk at PwC, customer experience analytics at Cisco and healthcare data compliance at Datavant, after earning degrees in public health and information systems from the University of Maryland. That background shapes how I build AI systems: in regulated settings, an answer has to be grounded in the source, not just confident.

Outside work, I DJ in New York and built my own software to organize my music library.
